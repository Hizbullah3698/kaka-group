Our Service Process

You approve the work before we touch the car.

Seven stages, each one logged against your job card.

Stage 01

Inspection

Vehicle received, condition photographed and the reported symptoms recorded.

Stage 02

Diagnosis

Scanned and tested until we find the actual cause, not the first plausible guess.

Stage 03

Approval

Written estimate with parts and labour separated. No work starts without your yes.

Stage 04

Repair

Carried out by a technician qualified on that system, with genuine or OE parts.

Stage 05

Quality Check

A second technician verifies the work independently of whoever performed it.

Stage 06

Final Testing

Road tested under the conditions the fault appeared in, then rescanned.

Stage 07

Vehicle Delivery

Cleaned, handed over in person, with the old parts available if you want them.
