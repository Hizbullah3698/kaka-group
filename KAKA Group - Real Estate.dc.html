Why Invest in Dubai

A market built for owners, not spectators.

Dubai combines a tax position most cities cannot offer with genuine tenant demand and a regulator that records every transaction. We advise on where those three things overlap.

0% No personal income taxRental income and capital gains are not taxed personally in the UAE, which changes the arithmetic on every holding.
6–9% Typical gross rental yieldsWell-selected apartments in established communities have historically outperformed most global gateway cities on yield.
3.9M+ Residents and growingPopulation growth, new-business formation and long-term visas keep occupancy tight across the mid and prime segments.
2 hrs To a third of the worldDubai International connects the emirate to the Gulf, South Asia, Europe and Africa, which is why tenancy demand here is international.
Freehold Foreign ownership securedRegistered freehold title in designated communities, with every transfer recorded by the Dubai Land Department.
