Maintenance & Reliability

A vehicle off the road is an order that never left.

For a delivery business, maintenance is availability. We run servicing on a schedule set by mileage and shift hours, in our own workshop, so a repair does not become a negotiation with a third party.

Scheduled servicing

Intervals set per vehicle by mileage and hours, booked outside your peak windows.

Preventive maintenance

Brakes, tyres, fluids and chains checked before they become roadside failures.

Safety inspections

Pre-deployment and periodic checks documented against each unit.

Quick repairs & replacement

Same-day turnaround where possible, and a replacement vehicle when it is not.
